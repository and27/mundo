# Prompts de las escenas · Yachay y la sombra que se movía

Herramienta: OpenAI, una sola para las 10 escenas, a ser posible todas en la misma conversación.
En **cada** escena adjunta: la ficha de Yachay (`docs/personajes/yachay-ficha-v3.webp`) y la ficha de la abuela.
Pega primero el **bloque fijo** y luego el de la escena.

## Bloque fijo (va en todas)

```
Use the characters from the attached reference sheets exactly.
Yachay: baby puma cub, big head, green eyes, thick dark brown eyebrows, white muzzle,
sandy-golden fur with the same 7 soft brown body spots, tail with two faint rings and a
dark brown tip. Clean face (no freckles), no collar, no necklace, no accessories.
Abuela: elderly adult puma, about twice Yachay's size, plain tan fur with no spots,
grey muzzle and brows, kind droopy green eyes, striped Andean shawl (warm red, mustard, teal).

Art style: 2D hand-painted watercolor children's picture book illustration on textured
cream paper, loose slightly wobbly brown ink outlines, soft uneven washes, colors gently
bleeding outside the lines. Not 3D, not digital, not glossy.

Format: vertical 9:16, full-bleed. The bottom 30% fades into plain cream watercolor paper
(no important details there), like a picture book page, for the text. Main characters in
the middle third. No text, no letters, no watermark.
```

## Escena 1 · Yachay (de día)
```
A sunny morning high in the Ecuadorian Andes. Grassy páramo slope with golden paja grass,
small orange chuquiragua flowers and round grey stones. On the right, the entrance of a small
cozy cave with a woven blanket hanging at the doorway. Far background: a soft snowy volcano
and puffy clouds, empty sky (no birds). Yachay, joyful, mid-leap between stones, mouth open
in a playful little roar, tail up. Empty space in front of him (left) for a butterfly.
Warm, bright palette.
```
*Efectos: mariposa animada, Kuntur cruza el cielo, pelusas de sol y paso al anochecer.*

## Escena 2 · La sombra
```
Inside their small cozy cave at night, soft periwinkle and lavender washes (not dark or scary),
warm accents: red patterned blanket, mustard pillow, little shelf with clay pots.
Yachay tucked in a round woven basket bed, wide curious eyes looking at the wall, tail raised
up behind him. Abuela sits beside him on a small wooden stool; a lit candle in a clay holder
on the floor next to her. A pale-yellow beam of moonlight enters from a round opening at the
top left, passes behind Yachay and casts a long curly tail-shaped shadow on the wall
(the shadow of his own tail).
```
*Efectos: la vela se apaga, la cueva se oscurece, polvo en el rayo y la sombra se mueve.*

## Escena 3 · El cuerpo avisa
```
Same cave at night, closer view. Yachay sitting up in his basket, scared but cute: whiskers
stiff and straight, ears back, eyes very wide, both paws pressed on his round tummy, mouth
open calling out. Behind him on the wall, the curly tail shadow in the moonbeam.
Abuela is not in the frame (she is coming). Candle is out (thin smoke wisp at most).
```
*Efectos: latido (la cámara pulsa con el "pum-pum") y bigotes con "tuiing".*

## Escena 4 · ¿Cuánto miedo?
```
Same cave at night. Abuela has come back and sits close beside the basket, leaning down to
Yachay's eye level, calm and gentle, listening. Yachay holds up one front paw clearly toward
the viewer showing FOUR toes raised, thumb tucked down. The paw must be large, clear and
easy to read. Soft moonlight on both faces.
```
*Efectos: los dedos se cuentan uno por uno (1… 2… 3… 4) con un brillo suave.*

## Escena 5 · Dónde se siente
```
Same moment, medium close-up. Yachay with one paw on his tummy and his whiskers slightly
stiff; Abuela gently touches her own chest with her paw, showing where she feels things.
Tender, quiet, safe. Soft moonlight.
```
*Efectos: brillo tibio en la panza y pausa con silencio para el niño.*

## Escena 6 · La frase
```
Close, tender composition. Abuela caresses Yachay's forehead with her paw, eyes half closed
(tender expression from her sheet). Yachay looks up at her, calmer. Warm, intimate, still
moonlight. Simple background.
```
*Efectos: acercamiento muy lento; todo se calma.*

## Escena 7 · La sombra copiona
```
Same cave. Yachay sitting up in his basket, curious, swishing his tail to the side; on the
wall in the moonbeam, the shadow of his tail copies the exact same curl. Yachay starts to
giggle (laughing expression from his sheet). Abuela in the background, smiling softly.
```
**Variante B** (para animar la cola): *la misma imagen, pero la cola y su sombra inclinadas hacia el otro lado.*
*Efectos: alterno A y B para que la cola y su sombra "bailen".*

## Escena 8 · Quedarse
```
Same cave, wider view. Yachay alone in his basket in the foreground, awake, a little tense
but staying, blanket pulled up. In the background, still clearly visible and close
("aquí cerquita"), Abuela sits in her own corner by the shelf, calm. The candle stays OUT.
Softer moonbeam.
```
*Efectos: el rayo baja de intensidad y la respiración se hace más lenta.*

## Escena 9 · Dormir
```
Same cave, peaceful. Yachay curled up asleep in his basket (sleepy pose from his sheet),
one small paw relaxed near his face. Abuela dozing in her corner in the background. A few
stars visible through the round opening. Very calm, soft lavender night.
```
*Efectos: estrellas que titilan y respiración del sueño.*

## Escena 10 · El truco de Yachay (mañana)
Antes, haz la **ficha de Kuntur** (ver abajo) y adjúntala también.
```
Morning outside the cave, soft golden light, páramo grass and stones. Kuntur, a young Andean
condor (from his reference sheet), perched on a rock. Yachay, cheerful, shows Kuntur his
raised paw with fingers up, explaining his trick. Kuntur lifts one wing, ready to try it.
```
*Efectos: luz de mañana y Kuntur levanta el ala.*
